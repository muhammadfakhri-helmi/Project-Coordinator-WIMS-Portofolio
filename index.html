02 The original problem

The challenge was not collecting more files. It was turning those files into a reliable coordination system.

Every well visit ends in an Excel Final Inspection report: general information, pressure observations, visual inspection and valve maintenance for each Christmas-tree valve, leak tests and a handover. Reports arrive from different wells, platforms and crews, one workbook at a time.

03 Final Inspection Inbox

Drop the report into the inspection inbox. WIMS begins tracking it automatically.

  1. 1

    Reports are placed into the Final Inspection Inbox

    A monitored folder is the single entry point. No one has to remember to upload a file.

  2. 2

    WIMS detects incoming workbooks

    The folder is watched for Excel files. Temporary lock files are ignored and a workbook is only read once it has finished copying.

  3. 3

    Processing begins automatically

    Each workbook is parsed and saved locally, one after another, with visible progress.

  4. 4

    The report becomes part of the tracked workflow

    From here it can be validated, counted, searched, reviewed and planned against — across hundreds of inspection reports.

04 Automation with human quality control

Automation where the data is reliable. Human review where judgment matters.

Spreadsheet cells are parsed into structured inspection fields. Each record then takes one of two paths.

Green path · validated automatically

Well identity matches the master well list and the fields are readable. The record enters WIMS with no review step.

Amber path · manual confirmation required

The well name is ambiguous or not in the master list, or fields are unreadable. That file is paused while the remaining files keep scanning.

Manual mapping is an intentional quality-control gate, not a system failure. As Project Coordinator I keep the final say over uncertain well identities and incomplete information — WIMS proposes a candidate, a person confirms it.

05 Operational dashboard

Hundreds of reports become one coordinated operational view.

Validated records are counted once, in one place, with the scoring rules written on the screen: Lock Open, Pneumatic and Production Choke valves stay in the reports but are excluded from every score and percentage.

07 Crew performance and work-quality review

Validated inspection records support fair review and continuous work-quality improvement.

Because every valve outcome is tied to a validated report, crew results can be discussed with evidence rather than impressions.

08 Searchable report dictionary

One searchable source for every inspection report.

Every Final Inspection file is registered with its well, platform, inspection date, scope, source and anomaly summary. In WIMS, authorised users can trace a record back to its workbook, view it and download the original.

09 Visit history and planning

Historical inspection records become the basis for the next field priority.

WIMS groups every visit by well and calculates the next due date from the latest valid inspection. It supports planning and recommendations; the Project Coordinator remains responsible for final prioritisation and operational decisions.

10 Performance trend

Every imported inspection updates the wider well integrity performance picture.

After each import, WIMS recalculates the cumulative passed percentage from 1 January to the latest inspection. Early in the year a single report moves the line; as records accumulate, the line shows the real level of performance.
